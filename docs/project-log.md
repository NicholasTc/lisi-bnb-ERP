Milestone 1:
PostgreSQL with reproducible migrations and separate development/test databases.
Employee authentication, multiple roles and server-side access controls.
Eight-hour session limits, thirty-minute inactivity expiry, logout and employee revocation.
Persistent login throttling, environment safeguards and sanitized errors.
Automated testing and database failure/recovery checks.

Concern: need a way for the owner to craete account easily for the employee

Milestone 2: menu & location setup 
Deliverables (determined by agent):
Owner creates and edits basic menu items and integer-IDR prices.
Authorized preparation staff toggle availability.
Customers browse the menu without logging in.
Court QR links select a validated building/court, with customer confirmation and override.

Future plan:
Milestone 3: Ordering with demo payments. 
Guest and counter orders, cart, checkout, secure order-status access, frozen prices and duplicate-request protection. Payments are clearly simulated.

Milestone 4: Employee operations and account management.
Preparation/delivery queues, exclusive claiming, pickup/dine-in handoff, alerts, service pauses, supervised exceptions, and an easy owner-controlled way to create/disable employee accounts.

Milestone 5: Reporting and reconciliation
Sales history, payments/refunds, daily totals and best sellers, with checks against double counting. Demo figures remain clearly separated.

Milestone 6: Real payment integration
Connect the selected QRIS provider; verify payments, handle duplicate/late notifications, reconcile transactions and execute full refunds safely.

Concern: but we need to test that the system will be able to capture payment data right 

Milestone 7: Production readiness
Secure production setup, backups and restore drills, outage/manual procedures, monitoring, security/concurrency checks and staff training.

Milestone 8: Limited pilot, then launch
Run controlled real orders, reconcile every payment, resolve observed issues, and expand only after agreed launch criteria pass.
